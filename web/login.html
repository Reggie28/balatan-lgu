Note on location access: browsers only share your location on a secure page. Signing in works on the computer running the system (http://127.0.0.1:5000) or via an HTTPS address — if you open the app from another device over plain Wi-Fi (http://192.168.…), the browser will refuse to share your location and sign-in will not proceed.
